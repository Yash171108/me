# ⚡ Reflex Speed Trainer

A high-quality, responsive web application designed to test and train your reaction time and reflex speed.

## Features

### 🎮 Interactive Game
- Click when the button appears on the screen
- Measures your reaction time in milliseconds
- Random delays (1-4 seconds) and button positions for variety
- Real-time feedback on your performance

### 📊 Comprehensive Statistics
- **Best Record**: Your fastest reaction time
- **Average Time**: Mean of all your attempts
- **Total Attempts**: Number of games played
- **Consistency Score**: Stability of your performance (0-100%)
- **Progress Level**: Track your improvement journey

### 📈 Detailed Progress Tracking
- **Recent Attempts Timeline**: Visual chart of your last 20 attempts
- **Improvement Level**: Progress bar showing skill advancement
- **Last 10 Attempts**: Detailed list with timestamps and ratings
- **Performance Ratings**: Classified as Excellent (<250ms), Good (<350ms), Average (<500ms), or Slow

### 💾 Data Management
- **Local Storage**: Automatically saves all your attempts
- **Export Data**: Download your complete statistics as JSON
- **Reset Stats**: Clear all data and start fresh

### 📱 Responsive Design
- Works seamlessly on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices
- Modern dark theme with gradient effects
- Smooth animations and transitions
- Touch-friendly controls

## Getting Started

1. Open `index.html` in your web browser
2. Click "Start Game" to begin
3. Wait for the green button to appear
4. Click as fast as you can when it appears
5. View your reaction time and statistics

## Performance Levels

| Reaction Time | Level | Progress |
|---|---|---|
| < 150ms | 🏆 Legend | 100% |
| 150-200ms | Expert | 95% |
| 200-250ms | Advanced | 85% |
| 250-300ms | Intermediate | 70% |
| 300-400ms | Novice | 50% |
| 400-500ms | Beginner | 30% |

## Ratings

- **⚡ Excellent** (<250ms): Lightning Fast! Incredible reflexes!
- **✨ Good** (<350ms): Great! Very quick reaction!
- **👍 Average** (<500ms): Not bad! Room for improvement!
- **🐢 Slow** (≥500ms): Need more practice! Keep training!
